Static Schedules, Dynamic Occupancy

Same 22°C setpoint for an empty room at midnight and a 15-person meeting at noon. The BMS has no awareness of who's actually there.

📋

The Facilities Ticket Trap

Occupants must raise a helpdesk ticket to change temperature. Average response time: hours to days. Meeting ends before it's fixed.

🌡️

Sensors Miss "Feels-Like"

Fanger's PMV (the international comfort standard) needs 6 variables: air temp, radiant temp, air speed, humidity, activity, clothing. Wall sensors provide 1 of 6.

🔌

Energy Workarounds Make It Worse

Occupants block AC vents, use personal space heaters — actively defeating the HVAC system and increasing energy consumption.

Why T_mass Matters

On a hot afternoon, walls stay warm for 2–4 hours after AC turns on. Air cools fast, but occupants still feel hot due to radiant heat from warm walls. Our 2R1C model captures this lag. Traditional single-node models don't.
